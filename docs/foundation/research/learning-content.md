## Current official specification and consequences

All links below were retrieved 2026-11-04. Official pages may still use historical terms such as “paper”; distinguish test construct from delivery mode.

- [IELTS Academic overview](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test): four skills; the Academic/General difference is Reading and Writing. Listed duration is 2h45 overall; Listening ~30 minutes, Reading 60, Writing 60, Speaking 11–14. This does not authorise substituting shortened source recordings for a full test.
- [Official Listening format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening): four parts, ten questions each, everyday contexts then education/training, recordings once, multiple accents. Word/number limits vary; over-limit gap responses lose the mark, hyphenated words count as one. [British Council teaching guidance](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/sites/default/files/listening_part_3_matching_questions.pdf) distinguishes two minutes checking on computer from ten minutes transfer on paper. Source selection and original audio production must retain preparation/checking pauses and natural connected speech, not just dialogue text.
- [Official Academic Reading format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading): three sections, 2,150–2,750 words in total, 40 questions, 60 minutes. Texts target a non-specialist audience, including detailed argument. A short passage exercise is legitimate practice but cannot be equated to that full construct by scaling its accuracy percentage.
- [Official Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing): two tasks in 60 minutes; Task 1 visual/process description, at least 150 words, recommended ~20 minutes; Task 2 connected argument, at least 250 words, ~40 minutes, double Task 1 weight. Do not invent a mechanical automatic band deduction for short text: the current page explains that short responses may provide insufficient evidence. Separate non-submission, insufficient evidence and an assessable response.
- [Official Speaking format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking): three parts, 11–14 minutes; interview 4–5, long turn 3–4 including one-minute preparation, discussion 4–5. A fixed prerecorded interviewer is useful rehearsal but does not reproduce a live examiner's interaction. A transcript cannot establish pronunciation and loses pause/prosody evidence.
- [Official scoring](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail): each correct Listening/Reading answer earns one mark; published raw thresholds are averages that vary by form. Writing's four criteria are equally weighted within each task; Speaking's four are equally weighted across performance. Overall is the mean of four section bands with quarter/three-quarter rounding upward. Public average thresholds do not supply an exhaustive calibrated lookup for new Mitooni forms.
- [Current Writing descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf) are nine pages, updated May 2023; use those rather than the local 2005 table. [Current Speaking descriptors](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) are four pages. Version the rubric, retain public source/date, and have Mo verify any Persian explanation against the English construct. No invented “percentage toward band 9” is supplied by those descriptors.
- [British Council delivery update](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/ielts-recognising-organisations/updates-ielts-test-delivery) says transition to computer from mid-2026 varies by market, with Writing on Paper in selected markets. Do not promise a paper-mode replica based only on older books. Confirm the pilot learners' intended test mode and destination; this review does not establish Iranian test-centre availability.

An attempted IDP preparation URL was inaccessible; another IDP result returned 403. The timing distinction above is supported by the British Council primary PDF/search result rather than pretending those inaccessible pages were read. No secondary commercial preparation sites are relied upon.
