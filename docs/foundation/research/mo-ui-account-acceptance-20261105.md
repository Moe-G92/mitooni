## Design challenge and acceptance contract

The proposed SDK AuthPort + Bearer BFF `/api/account` and narrow CAS/idempotent profile endpoints are appropriate beneath Mo's views. Nullable profile before onboarding, trusted membership, read-only identity email and light-only rendering are necessary adaptations, not a reason to replace Mo's shell or build a new visual kit.

Concrete acceptance probes, to execute after workers declare stable:

- Fresh Persian/RTL; anonymous English selection persists without private requests; legacy/corrupt preferences cannot force dark or arbitrary locale. Onboarding has no fabricated profile/name/goal, supports schema-agreed band range and retains Mo's two-step hierarchy. Capture sign-in/onboarding/settings/ready-capability states at 360/768/1280/1920 in relevant locales.
- Identity-only `/me` does not grant Mitooni membership. Account returns none/inactive with null profile and access-required, active/no-profile setup-required and completed ready. Only account/settings slice is enabled; unsupported learning/coach/payment/SMS/social capability must be visibly unavailable rather than succeeding through a sample handler.
- Session initialization/recovery/signout/refresh/account replacement are separate states. A GET, PATCH and refresh finishing after B login cannot commit A's data/error/draft or resend A's operation with B's token. Offline signout hides private state immediately, cancels/invalidates pending work and cannot resurrect through late response or reload. Preserve safe language preference.
- Distinguish 401 after bounded same-subject refresh, 403 membership/permission, 409 conflict, 422 validation, invalid JSON/schema and 503/network. 503 preserves valid session and edits with retry; 409 preserves draft and offers explicit canonical reconciliation; forbidden membership does not masquerade as invalid credentials. An older idempotent outcome must not replace a newer profile revision.
- Old prototype MSW worker is a special migration boundary: unregistering it alone does not release its current controller. Establish a test by loading prototype first, then the account app at the same origin; no live account/API request may occur under the stale controller. Either controlled safe reload or a visible blocking recovery is acceptable. Production fixture absence alone cannot prove this browser-state boundary.
- Production build graph and public asset admission exclude MSW worker/reset, prototype/sample auth/results, canned marking and fixture identity. Test query/localStorage toggles and direct prototype paths. Missing target config emits no provider/API request. Declared local fixture AuthPort plus real local HTTP may be used for acceptance, clearly marked; it must not enter production.
- Direct real isolated HTTP/PG checks: trusted synthetic A/B onboarding and PATCH; wrong expected actor, missing/inactive permissions, unknown nested fields and mass-assignment; same-revision concurrent writer; same operation replay versus changed payload; newer revision followed by old replay; membership revocation. Fresh API/repository/PG restart reads exact acknowledged values. No SQLite or in-memory result substitutes for this gate.
- UI: compare actual Mo baseline components/tokens/brand/light appearance, allowing only justified name/email/setup/error/capability states. Keyboard field progression, save/retry/conflict/dialog paths; four-width no internal/document clipping; text/non-text contrast and visible focus; axe zero serious/critical on affected states. Existing baseline defects are recorded rather than blindly inherited as acceptable.
