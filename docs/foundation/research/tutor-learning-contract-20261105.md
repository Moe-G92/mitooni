## Bounded outcome

The AI is the primary teaching actor: establish interpretation → explain using approved anchors → ask a purposeful check → wait for an attempt → give evidence-linked formative feedback → elicit repair/revision → propose the next allowed action. Teacher-designed objectives, content and rules govern this loop. Human coaching adds optional interpretation/prescription; policy-required expert assessment remains separately attributable.

The engineering slice represents U2 Reading evidence finding and U3 Writing Task 2 argument development, with prerequisite support and challenge branches. It does **not** implement the complete U1–U6 curriculum, Listening/Speaking, a full mock, level placement, mastery estimator or band prediction. Mo's current study interface owns visual structure. Internal enums/reasons in this contract should become concise essential actions/statuses, not a diligence screen.

A published synthetic fixture means “available in an isolated test catalog”; it never means teacher-approved learner content. The production content gate must reject fixtures even if an operator misconfigures the catalog. No new reviewer booking, live-coach service, capacity or turnaround promise is inferred.

## Pedagogic states and permitted actions

Keep pedagogic stage separate from transport (`saving`, `saved`, `generation_pending`, `generation_failed`) and human review (`not_required`, `not_requested`, `pending`, `returned`, `unavailable`). Waiting for feedback does not erase saved work or lock unrelated available learning.

| Stage | AI teaching action | Required server guard / resulting evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orient | Ask how the learner interprets the objective/instructions; offer suitable support or challenge | Authorized available edition; record branch choice, not inferred CEFR/band |
| Explain/check | Retrieve permitted anchors, explain one manageable point, ask an unscored check | Teaching-policy scope; record actual delivery/exposure and assistance. Dynamic check is formative, not a newly approved marked item |
| Attempt open | Open an approved task and wait; provide permitted controls only in independent conditions | Server resolves eligibility, freezes condition/version bundle and creates attempt. No model-generated assessment item |
| Submitted | Interpret released raw marks or evidence; ask learner to justify Reading evidence / review a Writing decision | Server freezes response before key/feedback release. Output references exact submitted revision |
| Repair/revise | Explain released rationale, prompt specific correction, compare revisions cautiously | Assistance recorded; same-family revision is development evidence, not fresh independent transfer |
| Next action proposed | Suggest support, retry learning, fresh development check, next objective, revisit, or optional expert review | Server validates approved graph, availability, evidence, exposure, permissions and expected revision before committing |
| Paused/resumable | Acknowledge last saved work; retry unfinished action honestly | Persisted run/attempt wins over model memory; retries cannot create a second submission or review request |

The tutor may vary explanation length, Persian/English explanation and scaffold use within approved policy. It cannot rewrite keys/rubrics, publish content, mark a family unseen, grant a role, assign a coach, declare validated mastery or emit a learner-facing numeric AI rubric/band estimate. Deterministic server raw marks are permitted after submission under the appropriate key; “1 of 1 on this synthetic practice item” is not proficiency evidence. No four-skill composite from two tasks.

## Supported work versus independent evidence

Record conditions rather than one misleading success flag. `evidence_use` distinguishes supported practice, same-family revision, fresh-family developmental independent work and sealed final evaluation. Even eligible independent work supports only the stated objective/demand/condition, not a general ability conclusion.

Exposure ledger records family/edition, owner, exposure type and source event/time. Events include stimulus shown, teaching anchor/rationale delivered, hint/model answer released, response attempted and earlier known source familiarity self-report. Log content release on the server before/atomically with authorizing delivery; an unacknowledged client render does not prove unseen. Existing exposure cannot be undone by deleting a chat, signing out, changing locale or changing a task ID. Delivery/render uncertainty is conservatively ineligible for unseen use until resolved, never default-unseen. Do not expose sealed families to tutoring retrieval even after another family is submitted.

Independent start requires a **fresh restricted context**: only permitted stimulus/instructions/control help, with no old tutoring transcript, private key/rationale or sibling-family teaching. Server checks actual family history and the context/tool manifest. General learner history remains saved outside that context. Record prior familiarity if reported; unknown imported history cannot justify a “verified unseen” claim. An independent-context flag without these checks is insufficient.

During independent work the learner may request help. Before a hint/translation/model is released, explicitly switch to supported learning if allowed, record assistance and invalidate independent eligibility; preserve work. If policy prohibits the switch (sealed evaluation), offer exit/save according to policy without revealing teaching content. Accessibility support is recorded by functional condition; it does not automatically imply low ability. Interface/control clarification is distinguished from substantive language/task help by approved policy; ambiguous help requests abstain/offer learning mode.

A fresh developmental check subsequently used to teach repair becomes development evidence, not a reusable sealed final-evaluation item. Final holdouts are separate by family. Server checks must span editions/sibling variants, not merely “no previous attempt on task ID”.

## Focused lifecycle and output-release agreement

After the first handoff, frontend/backend challenge made the lifecycle concrete: admission creates an OPEN attempt, durable draft and restricted context **before** protected stimulus/context release. Submission freezes that existing attempt; it is not the first independent-eligibility check. Own permitted stimulus exposure while resuming the same open attempt is distinguishable from outside/pre-admission familiarity or prohibited later assistance. Explicit learner-only help switching appends conditions/exposure history and irreversibly downgrades the open attempt before any hint release. After submission, teaching/revision creates a supported child attempt; it does not rewrite the original frozen as-of eligibility snapshot. Later exposure still affects future fresh-family eligibility.

## Feedback and progression authority

AI feedback is labelled `ai_formative`, with policy/model/run identity when implemented, grounded content references and actual response evidence. Teacher-approved source/rubric is not a claim that each generated sentence was expert approved. Missing response evidence, invalid citations or uncertain interpretation yields abstention/retry/review, not invented certainty. Text quoted from learner work is data, never permission to invoke an unauthorized tool.

A human feedback record requires a current authorized reviewer actor and assignment, submitted revision, rubric version and explicit provenance; a model cannot supply `human_reviewed=true`. Revoked/reassigned reviewers cannot read or submit. An expert correction supersedes with lineage rather than overwriting AI/human history. A late review attaches only to its assigned frozen submission. Human numeric scoring, if later enabled, needs its own reviewed policy; it does not validate model scores.

The next-step validator is deterministic and bounded. Check: (1) active member/ownership/current run revision; (2) target exists, available and within approved objective graph; (3) requested action permitted in current stage; (4) evidence IDs belong to learner, match versions/conditions and are not withdrawn/ineligible; (5) prerequisite condition or permitted learner challenge/support alternative; (6) new-family restrictions; (7) human-review dependency where actually required. Return accepted transition or a stable reason plus available alternatives. Rejection does not ask the model to invent a bypass.

For this synthetic slice, safe rules can accept `offer_fresh_check` after an attempt plus evidence-linked repair, or `offer_next_objective` with an explicit learner choice. They do not assert that the objective has been mastered. Domain-approved continuation rules remain a later exact-version artifact. No hidden “80%”, token-count, chat fluency or universal repetition threshold. A learner can decline a suggestion, revisit support or challenge available work without losing unrelated evidence. An authorized coach can prescribe available objective/support with reason and review point; cannot override ownership, exposure, key embargo or score validity. If coaching is unavailable/not assigned, expose honest status and suitable AI-guided work, not a fake coach response.

## Failure and recovery rules

Version conflict retains draft and requires explicit canonical refresh/merge choice. No published suitable target offers a reviewed available alternative or honest unavailable state; never generate a replacement marked task or consume a holdout. Invalid/empty work yields insufficient/unsubmitted evidence, not zero ability. Model failure leaves last authoritative step and saved pack/work available, with retry; static material is fallback recovery, not the primary teaching design. Duplicate late model output cannot attach to a newer response revision. Missing grounded evidence yields abstention. Human delays permit other safe AI work while review status remains honest. All errors distinguish unavailable generation from failed save: never say “saved” until acknowledged. Frontend can preserve an account-bound unsaved draft according to accepted local policy, but must not replay it under another account.
