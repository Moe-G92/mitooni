## Observed reusable mechanisms

**A producer is not its own reviewer.** `backend/app/services/generated_question_reviewer.py:1429` rejects equal canonical author/reviewer model identities, with request-layer alias backstops evidenced in `backend/tests/test_generated_question_reviewer.py` test names around lines 1563–1615. `build_reviewer_prompt` at line 574 includes the candidate, review contract and teaching context, without author self-check or harness scores. `ReviewerReport` and `apply_review_decision` distinguish approval, rejection and pending revision; approval also requires a version-bound explanation contract. A different model reduces a particular dependence but does not establish empirical independence or teacher competence.

**Session isolation is stronger than a role label.** Archive `stage2lib/store.py:275–300` binds jobs to session, role, attempt and content hash; it denies previously exposed sessions blind work, denies pack editors their own review, and denies candidate authors their own generated review. `stage2lib/context.py:230` and `:257` deny source retrieval/navigation in blind phases. A solver can continue into an explicitly unblinded diagnosis/review after its immutable answer is collected. This is a useful controlled reveal, not a second blind measurement.

**Committed evidence survives correction.** Archive `stage2lib/delivery.py:220` stores versioned drafts, validates before commit, treats an identical resubmission idempotently and rejects replacement of a validated final submission. `workflow.py:321` collects only a matching leased session/attempt with compatible policy and unchanged sheet. `workflow.py:280` permits blind-evidence reuse only for unchanged sheet and candidate blind hash/epoch; explanation-only work still renews source review. Separate hashes answer separate questions: duplicate content, assessment-visible content and complete publication content are not interchangeable.

**An unresolved finding is an explicit state.** Archive `workflow.py:428–460` requires all lead review dimensions with reasons, resolved finding dispositions and source-backed corrective instructions for conflicts. `workflow.py:491–518` refuses an approved item carrying unresolved findings or required changes, refuses deterministic failure overrides, requires dispositions for review flags and an explanation of the committed blind outcome. A revision routes to repair; unresolved work becomes `needs_input`. The evidence supports explicit hold and repair, not a generic majority-vote adjudicator.

**Completion and release are separate.** `generated_review_gates.py` defaults to complete approval and zero pending, rejected, quarantined, missing or error rates. Empty denominators yield `insufficient_data`. `canary_release_verdict.py` requires both scored teaching assessment and completed item review to pass; pending or absent reports cannot become acceptable. Archive `stage2.py:16` exports readiness candidates and promotion dependencies, and the runbook explicitly says export does not publish. Rejected drafts may be retained privately, but a release manifest should contain only its intended approved units, with planned coverage rechecked after exclusions.

**Serving applies a second gate.** `backend/app/services/generated_questions.py:1973` checks generated origin, approved lifecycle, assessment kind, retirement/quarantine, serving timestamp, structure, provenance, duplicate check, authority review and explanation attestation. These five-option and readable-length rules are SQE-specific and must not be copied to IELTS. `supabase/migrations/20261022160000_explanation_contract_attestation_guard_v1.sql:75` removes stale explanation attestations when content no longer matches its hash; repair staging/audit tables deny ordinary client access. This pattern prevents a content edit retaining a prior valid badge.

**Published content and learner ownership require database boundaries.** `backend/app/services/study_editions.py` pins first-open editions with conflict-safe insert/readback, exact pack lookup and stale-write 409 responses. `supabase/migrations/20261104010602_study_editions_expand_v1.sql` uses versioned foreign keys, immutable published flashcard updates, expected-version switches and transactional grading/mark guards. `20261104123722_study_edition_ownership_atomic_v1.sql` places ownership-history insertion in a trigger rather than depending on every application writer and bounds its one-time historical repair. ## Proposed Mitooni adaptation

Use the smallest independent content package and harness, integrated with Mitooni's reviewed tutor/attempt contracts. Do not import the SQE controller as a second platform or inherit its domain prompts, model defaults, licensed source adapters, numerical floors or question templates. No new model calls are authorized by this record.

The teacher-owned roadmap maps each learning objective to prerequisites, instruction, guided demonstration, independent practice, feedback/retry, delayed retrieval, assessment and escalation. Keep the whole IELTS Academic roadmap visible to teacher review even when only representative units are authored. A curriculum node needs a coverage disposition (`first-set`, `planned`, `held`) and a reason; blank nodes must not appear complete. Foundation, developing and advanced learner pathways should be represented as support and progression choices, not unvalidated score conversions.

Each original unit should bind: unit ID/version; objective and roadmap version; skill/task type; intended level/support; English content and Persian/English delivery fields; prerequisites; teacher notes; permitted source/provenance references; assessment/rubric version; expected response/key or teacher marking guide; feedback/retry rules; tutor allowed/forbidden actions; media/transcript hashes where applicable; author identity; independent review; unresolved findings; evaluation report; complete content hash. Provenance and answer keys remain private. Public learning material must not expose internal workflow labels.

Proposed lifecycle: `draft → schema_checked → independent_review → teacher_review → evaluation_ready → publication_candidate → published`; side states `changes_requested`, `held`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`. Only the reviewed exact version can advance. Human teacher review remains a distinct requirement for the first set; automated approval must not stand in for it. Optional human coaching is an actual escalation/delivery path, not a substitute for the teacher-designed AI sequence.

For disagreement, preserve each original verdict and evidence, hold only the affected objective/unit/dependent evaluations, and assign an independent teacher adjudicator. Record the issue, competing interpretations, evidence, decision, rationale, applicable versions and required retest. Author/reviser cannot adjudicate their own dispute; a majority vote or a successful solver answer cannot erase a material objection. Adjudication creates a new disposition or revision rather than rewriting old reviews. Global work need not stop when an unrelated unit is held.

Use separate review/evaluation lanes: (a) deterministic structure, media and key checks, (b) independent educational/content review without author confidence scores, (c) learner-like blind task execution without answer keys, (d) source-aware diagnosis after committed response, (e) teacher evaluation of explanations, scoring and escalation. Writing/speaking need teacher-authored analytic marking guides and synthetic response/audio anchors; MCQ accuracy cannot validate them. AI feedback is formative until separately validated; do not infer an overall band from short drills or unmarked skills.

Publication should create an immutable manifest containing exact unit, roadmap, marking-guide, prompt and media hashes, review/adjudication references, evaluation versions and approval identity. Promotion changes the current pointer atomically with expected preimage; attempts pin the manifest and scoring policy. Withdrawal stops future assignment while preserving seen content, answers and feedback history. Rollback restores prior assignment pointers without rewriting historical attempts. This defines an internal publication contract; Arash's external release approval is still separate.

### Objections sent to coordinator before implementation

**Package closure is underspecified.** The design hashes a manifest, ordered units and evaluation specification, while the chosen deliverable also requires a roadmap, four mock candidates, stimulus/media requirements and a review edition. Resolve and constrain all paths; reject symlinks at every component and do not permit output paths to overwrite inputs or follow symlinks.
