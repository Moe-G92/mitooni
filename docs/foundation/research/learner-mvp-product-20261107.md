## Proposed smallest complete learner journey

“Minimum complete” means every listed stage works on real persisted server state; it does not mean a complete textbook library or calibrated band prediction.

1. **Enter and resume.** Existing account sign-in→membership→profile remains. Returning learners land on Today/resumable work, never an internal review target. Reviewer-only routes stay role-restricted and outside learner navigation. Preserve existing three accounts; do not manufacture invitations.
2. **Onboard.** Add explicit Academic/General Training, retain targets/date/hours and language. Ask prior familiarity/current self-report only as self-report, not a verified score. Finish at baseline or Today. No deadline/target promise and no compulsory full mock before learning.
3. **Establish a baseline.** Offer four separate small skill samples and a familiarisation/support route. Record response, help, conditions and uncertainty per objective; permit skip/continue later and insufficient-evidence state. Begin with an accessible task, offer a harder/easier suitable sample without a universal band threshold. Speaking requires actual audio evidence; a typed answer cannot place pronunciation. A small baseline suggests a starting point, not an IELTS band. If a dedicated fresh diagnostic family is unavailable, label its purpose ordinary starting practice and do not claim validated placement or unseen evidence.
4. **Plan on Today.** A server-owned plan shows one next action, resumable work and a weekly allocation within stated time. All four skills remain available. Rank diagnosed prerequisite/repair needs, due revisits, target priorities and available time; use eligible source-grounded units. Preserve per-skill routing and learner choice. Zero weekly hours/unset date must still allow manual practice; do not divide by zero or fabricate a schedule. New users receive a purposeful starting plan even with no scores. An uncertain baseline triggers further sampling, not forced foundation classification.
5. **Prepare in Mo's skill room/workspace.** AI explains the selected source objective using admitted teaching anchors and learner work, asks a purposeful check, adjusts example/scaffold length/language, and fades help when warranted. A lesson must contain actual meaningful input and learner action; chat plus a generic prompt is insufficient. Read the actual chart/passage/audio/letter task. Adapt within versioned objective/track boundaries; never invent external source facts or silently change assessed construct.
6. **Practice.** Correct skill-specific player: multiple objective answers for Reading/Listening, Task1/Task2 writing, recorded Speaking with take management. Persist acknowledged drafts, audio and conditions; preview/confirm recording before submission. Deterministic marking stays server-side. Tutor hints/transcript/translation are explicit assistance, recorded before release.
7. **Feedback and repair.** Objective feedback refers to answer/evidence span or audio timecode. Productive feedback refers to actual text/audio and criterion with specific next action. Preserve original submission; repair creates a child revision. AI feedback, raw marks and actual human review remain distinct. A missing provider leaves a clear unavailable/retry state while saved work remains usable; deterministic or synthetic responses must never impersonate live AI. A provider must be configured and a real qualitative teaching journey demonstrated before claiming this requested AI MVP is complete.
8. **Fresh application.** Offer new-family transfer for the same objective if genuinely eligible; independent mode has no tutor context/answer help. Same-source adapted siblings do not become unseen by changing ID or wording. When no fresh family exists, allow openly repeated practice and show independent check unavailable; choose another eligible objective or reviewer escalation. No forged completion or mastery.
9. **Mock and review.** Track-specific Reading/Writing plus shared Listening/Speaking form a real version-pinned session with server-owned timing, section transitions, durable saves, media, submit/recovery and immutable responses. Speaking needs a reviewed responsive interviewer or an explicitly labeled rehearsal mode; fixed recordings cannot establish interactive mock completion. A full-form candidate with unavailable media/interaction is not an operational full mock. Result separates raw marks, pending productive review, formative feedback and human decisions. Never calculate an overall from missing skills or unvalidated numeric estimates.
10. **Progress and coach.** Mo's progress structure shows real completed work, objective evidence/conditions, per-skill needs, revisions and next repair/revisit. No dummy seeded trend or accuracy-to-band conversion. Request review binds immutable work to a named authorized coach; requested/in-review/returned states and actual replies persist. Unavailable coaching capacity has a real empty state, never simulated booking/reply. Returned feedback can replan with evidence and coach override reason. A review request is a product action within authenticated tooling, not authority to send external email/messages.

The learning product is blocked from an honest complete claim while live AI, operational full mocks or durable skill flows remain absent; these are implementation/evidence gaps to resolve, not reasons to narrow the deliverable back to the review panel.

## Personalization data and semantics

Reuse existing profile/account, immutable bundle and attempt/workspace identities. Add only what selection/teaching actually needs:

- **Learner context:** examTrack, target per skill, date/available time, interface/teaching language, self-report provenance, declared source familiarity, learner preferences and approved accessibility conditions. Self-report and goal are not measured ability.
- **Objective graph:** stable objective/version; source-level and exact source-anchor mapping; prerequisite links; applicable tracks/tasks; demand branch as an authoring property; teach/practice/repair/transfer options; teacher-defined evidence policy. Do not infer difficulty from filename, publisher target band or arbitrary percent.
- **Activity manifest:** exact public stimulus/prompt/question/media IDs and hashes, private keys/rationales, objective and criterion mapping per activity, source provenance, family and semantic groups, assistance options, expected duration as estimate, language/modality and review/publication state. Parse complete unit activities rather than truncate to one item.
- **Evidence event:** learner/attempt/work revision; objective/item or criterion; raw outcome or qualitative observation; cited work span/timecode; actor/model/provider/prompt/rubric versions; supported/independent/timed conditions; modality sufficiency; uncertainty; exposure; timestamp. Preserve contradictory and insufficient evidence; completion alone never means mastered.
- **Learner objective state:** evidence references, current support needs, tentative route/demand, latest independently eligible check, due revisit and reason. Keep a reproducible aggregation policy version. No fabricated calibrated proficiency number.
- **Plan revision:** candidate inputs/hash, eligible option IDs, chosen/alternative step, objective, supporting evidence IDs, reason code, expected time, due date, actor/override and policy version. Commit expected revision atomically; provider proposes, server validates publication/track/prerequisites/exposure/ownership and accepts. Date/target/answer/review changes cause explainable replan without rewriting old work.
- **Tutor context:** exact admitted objective, instructional anchors, track, declared conditions, relevant work/evidence and current plan stage. Supply private source interpretation only through the allowed authoring/serving boundary. Never send entire corpus, answer key, held-out mock, unrelated account history or authoring review record as ambient context. Model-generated additional learner-facing material must retain derivation/version and stay formative unless independently admitted.

Every candidate receives personalization, including a new/uncertain candidate: uncertainty is an input that produces appropriate sampling/support choices. Personalized wording alone is insufficient. Two candidates with different track/target/time must receive materially different priorities, lessons and feasible schedules with recorded reasons. Same profile with changed actual evidence must produce a different objective/repair choice where the evidence warrants it; same evidence and different track must select track-correct Reading/Writing; altered time budget must change feasible allocation; unsupported/unreviewed content cannot be selected to conceal exhaustion.
